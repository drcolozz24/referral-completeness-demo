# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository. Dates are Australian Eastern time.

## Unreleased — changes since v1.0 (to be tagged v1.1 once reviewed and merged)

### 2026-10-07 (link to the checker project)
- **Linked to the separate checker project.** The information panel and `llms.txt` now point to *Referral information check* (https://github.com/drcolozz24/referral-info-check), an experimental open-source checker that grew from this demonstration. The link says it is research on fictional letters only, not validated and not for real referrals. Nothing else on the page changed.

### 2026-10-07 (after v1.3)
- **Made findable by search engines.** The page and the front page now carry a description, a canonical address and sharing tags; `sitemap.xml` lists the page, the agent guide and the data file. Before this the site gave search engines only a bare title, and a search for the project's name did not find it. No visible content of the demonstration changed.

### 2026-10-07 (after v1.2)
- **Published for software and AI agents as well as people.** Added `data/nsw-chest-pain-adult.json` (the NSW criteria as structured data, with source, licence, the date on NSW's page and the date last verified) and `llms.txt` (a plain-text guide for agents: what this is, what it must not be used for, how to quote and attribute it). The data file is generated by `tests/build_data.py` from a snapshot parsed from NSW's HTML; the tests fail if it differs from the newest snapshot, and the monthly check rebuilds it only when the comparison is consistent. It includes NSW's emergency criteria, which the page does not show, so that an agent reading it cannot miss them. Nothing added here reads or assesses a referral letter.
- **Author's decision:** conditional items such as "Troponin (if indicated)" keep NSW's wording and are counted like any other item; whether the condition applies is the clinician's decision.

### 2026-10-07 (after v1.1)
- **Result screen states the result in a sentence and quotes NSW on the consequence.** The result now reads "This letter does not mention N of the 13 items NSW lists as required" (or "mentions all 13"), followed by three passages from NSW Health's FAQ on the state-wide referral criteria, with a link: that the required list is "Mandatory information that is to be supplied with referrals"; that where the criteria are not referenced, "patients may experience delayed access to care due to NSW public specialist outpatient services seeking additional clinical and/or referral information from referring health professionals to support referral screening and triage and/or returning the referral to referring health professionals with advice of alternative care options"; and that clinicians "may override the SRC should it be deemed necessary". The author asked for the result to show whether a referral would be accepted or rejected under the state pathway. NSW says a referral may be queried and/or returned but does not say which, so the page quotes NSW and does not predict a decision. The statement box is now neutral in colour.
- **A truncated quotation caught before publication.** The AI text-extraction tool had returned the second passage cut off after "referral information", omitting "and/or returning the referral". The draft built on that said NSW published no accept-or-decline rule, which understated what NSW says. The passages were corrected against the page's HTML, saved by the author, before anything was published.
- **Display fix.** Since v1.0 the progress shape on the result screen was drawn as a filled green wedge that covered the second half of the count ("5 /" was readable, "13" was not). It is now a ring with the count legible inside it. The page tests did not catch this because they read the text, not the picture.
- **Author's decisions on the audit's clinical points** (7 October 2026): the fictional case stays as it is; "Patient health summary" stays an item in its own right (total 13); "Troponin (if indicated)" keeps NSW's wording. Still open: how unmentioned conditional items are presented, and whether the ring reads as a score.

### 2026-10-07
- **First run of the monthly workflow.** It reached the NSW page from GitHub Actions and recorded its outcome as designed, but the outcome was "could not run": the parser, written against a mock-up, could not read the Emergency section or the third triage category (the NSW page nests lists directly inside lists and contains zero-width characters). `tests/fetch_nsw.py` corrected against the page's real HTML, saved from Chrome by the author.
- **First character-for-character check** against that HTML: Required list, If-available list, triage categories and date identical to the demo. Snapshot added (`nsw-chest-pain-adult-2026-10-07-saved-page.json`), now including the "Important information" paragraph.
- **Omission found and disclosed.** On the NSW page, "Cardiovascular disease risk assessment" carries a note: "refer to the Australian guideline and calculator for assessing and managing cardiovascular risk resource". The earlier snapshots, taken through an AI text-extraction tool, had dropped it, so the earlier "consistent" results did not cover it. The demo does not reproduce the note; the page and NOTICE now say so, the checker lists such notes, fails if the page stops saying so, and reports any change to them.

### 2026-10-06
- **Monthly check record.** The scheduled check on 1 October 2026 could not run (the fetch needed an approval nobody was present to give). A check run by hand on 6 October 2026 found the Required list, the If-available list, the three triage categories and the date identical to the NSW page. Both outcomes, and the 24 September alignment, are now recorded in `tests/fetched/CHECK-LOG.md`; a 6 October snapshot was added, which for the first time records NSW's Emergency section and page headings. The README previously said each outcome was recorded in this changelog; it was not, and the README now points to the log.
- **Second independent audit** (6 October 2026, by an AI agent given the repository and four parameters — autonomy, consistency, clinical appropriateness, truthfulness — and none of the first reviewer's conclusions; not a human review). Findings applied:
  - `tests/check_criteria.py` rewritten to compare character for character. It previously ignored case, spacing, dash type and trailing full stops, checked only the first occurrence of the date, passed when no date was found, and did not compare order, duplicates or sub-item structure. It now checks all of those, checks the date in the page, README and NOTICE, requires the triage lines, and compares NSW's headings and Emergency section between snapshots.
  - Added `tests/fetch_nsw.py` (downloads the NSW page, stores its SHA-256 hash and the relevant text as a snapshot) and two GitHub Actions workflows: the tests on every push, and a monthly check that records its outcome and opens an Issue on any difference or failure. The workflows never edit the page. The parser had not been run against the live page when added.
  - `tests/test_demo.js`: the check that the author's name is not in the fictional letter now inspects the letter (it did not before); the wording the page displays is compared with the newest snapshot; added checks for the default ticks, sub-item display, every navigation button, Escape, overflow on all four screens, the credit line, the licence link and the statement of changes. The README no longer states a number of checks.
  - README: test descriptions corrected; "Every version is tagged" reworded; "Every Australian state publishes…" changed to "At least five Australian states publish…", matching `docs/`.
  - docs/01: the two quotations from Osman et al. were checked against the article page and the "to be re-verified" flag replaced with what was checked and how; the remaining quotations are stated to be unverified since the original searches. docs/02: declaration amended to give the dates entries were actually read; link added for the RACGP guide. docs/04: "four state health departments" reworded to remove an inconsistent count.
- **Correction — other states' copyright.** The page and README described the Victorian, Queensland and Western Australian criteria as "Crown copyright", and NOTICE and docs/02 as "Crown copyright with all rights reserved". Neither phrase is on any of the three sites. Queensland Health's general statement is a Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives licence, with the criteria page itself requiring permission to reproduce. The wording now says the criteria are not published under an open licence that would permit reproduction here, and docs/02 quotes and links each site as read on 6 October 2026. The decision not to reproduce them is unchanged.
- **Attribution.** Page credit changed to "Created by Dr Ferney Bernal Buitrago, with AI assistance". The Creative Commons licence is now linked on the page, and the changes made to the NSW material are stated in full (sub-items listed individually; triage table shown as lines; no wording changed).
- **Company named.** The former company referred to under v1.0 below and in docs/03 was ReferRight; it is named with the author's agreement.
- These corrections were themselves reviewed by a further AI agent before being offered for merge; it found ten defects in the first draft (among them: the monthly job could lose its own record if a push failed; the parser dropped added paragraphs; several sentences described automation in the present tense before it had run). All were fixed.
- Not changed, and left for the author's clinical judgement: how close the fictional case sits to NSW's emergency criteria and whether to reproduce them; how conditional items ("if indicated") are counted; whether "Patient health summary" is counted alongside its sub-items; and whether the result screen's colours and ring read as a score.
